# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — the first release

A chess opponent with a coach beside you: the coloured box and its reasons, hints, the threat, hanging pieces, the
strongest line and its play-through; four coaching levels (Tournament, Play, Guided, Full coaching); ten opponent
strengths calibrated against lichess error rates; clocks and the game buttons; the move list with marks, comments and
your notes; the win-chance graph; PGN saving and opening; opening names, a book and opening practice; a board editor;
light and dark appearance; the built-in help with pictures. Linux AppImage; Windows as a preview. Buster 7.0.3
built in.
