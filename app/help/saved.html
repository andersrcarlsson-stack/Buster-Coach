Your saved games

Every game of 10 moves or more is saved by itself, as a PGN file — the standard chess format that other programs and websites (lichess, for example) can read — in Documents/Buster Coach, one folder per month. Your mistakes are marked "?" with the better move, your only good moves "!", every move has the coach's evaluation, and your own notes are kept with their moves. Switch it off under Settings → Save games automatically; Forget last game removes the last one saved.

Open game… (Ctrl+O) shows your games, newest first, with the final position. Open one to go through it move by move with its evaluations and comments; Play from here continues it as a new game from the move you're looking at. Any PGN file can be opened this way.
