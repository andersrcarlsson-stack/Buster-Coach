Playing a game

  • New game: the New buttons (♔ as White, ♚ as Black), or Game → New game. During a game, or in a position you set up, they ask for a second click, so nothing is lost by accident.
  • Time menu: untimed, or a time control (minutes + seconds added per move). A new choice applies from the next game. If your time runs out you lose, unless the opponent couldn't possibly mate with what it has left: then it's a draw. (The same rule applies to the opponent.)
  • Pause stops the clocks (in a timed game the board is hidden meanwhile). Play now makes the opponent move at once. Offer draw: the opponent accepts if it isn't better. Resign ends the game. Draw and Resign ask for a second click.
  • Take back (Ctrl+Z) takes back your last move, and the opponent's answer.
  • Look back at any earlier move: click it in the move list, or use the arrows beside it (← → Home End). The game goes on meanwhile; End or Esc brings you back.
  • Set up a position (Game → Set up position…) and play from it as White or Black.
  • The end of a game: a banner across the board, and the eval bar shows WON, LOST or DRAW.
